## What does it do?

Helps players navigate the Sanctum by calculating and visualizing the optimal path through rooms based on customizable weights for different room types, afflictions, rewards, and connectivity. Supports both **Keyboard/Mouse** and **Controller UI** modes, with automatic layout scaling and alignment adjustment.

Automatically updates weights as you progress through the Sanctum, taking into account your current position and providing a visual guide for the most beneficial route based on the profiles preferences and risk tolerance.
